# What each role does — daily to yearly (drives the home page « Mes actions »)

Sources: SIGAT pilot (three finance roles validated with the owner), Tunisian obligations
(monthly tax declaration, CNSS, corporate tax instalments, public-contract deadlines).
Exact dates and rates are **settings**, to confirm with the company's accountant.

Legend: ⚡ = alert/warning generated by the system · 🏠 = tile on the role's home page.

## Gérant (owner)
| Period | Work | System support |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Look at exceptions and decide (cash differences, overrides, blocked items); approve large payments | 🏠 « Décisions » (count, oldest), 🏠 « À approuver » ⚡ overdue approvals |
| Weekly | Cash position by bank / cash box / site; transit not confirmed; open tickets by person | 🏠 « Trésorerie » card, ⚡ transit > N days |
| Monthly | Close the month (after controller's checklist); review project costs and partner balances | 🏠 « Clôture du mois », ⚡ month not closed by day X |
| Quarterly | Groupement / partner statements; subcontractor balances; guarantees (cautions) to release | ⚡ caution release dates, RG to claim |
| Semester | Review controls and thresholds; who sees what | Settings screen « Règles et contrôles » |
| Yearly | Year-end with the accountant; corporate tax return; archive | ⚡ tax deadlines (settings) |

## Contrôle (controller — caisses et banque)
| Period | Work | System support |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Validate entries of others (never his own); check relevés de caisse; confirm receptions | 🏠 « À valider » (count, oldest), bulk validate |
| Weekly | Cash counts (PV de caisse) per cash box; follow missing receipts; chase transit | 🏠 « Comptages », ⚡ cash box not counted for 7 days |
| Monthly | Import bank statements; reconcile; month checklist (transit = 0, à identifier justified) | 🏠 « Rapprochement », ⚡ unmatched lines > N days |
| Quarterly | Subcontractor statements confirmation; loans / OB register review | ⚡ statements not confirmed |
| Yearly | Support year-end; certificates of withholding | Exports |

## Saisie et pièces (finance clerk)
| Period | Work | System support |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Enter operations (payments, receipts, transfers), attach receipts, fix refused entries | 🏠 « Nouveau » quick buttons; 🏠 « Refusés à corriger »; ⚡ missing receipt |
| Weekly | Enter site vouchers received on paper; replenishment requests | Grid paste; 🏠 « Relevés à saisir » |
| Monthly | Prepare tax and CNSS payments from the accountant's figures; supplier payment run | ⚡ due dates (monthly declaration ~ 21–28, CNSS) |
| Quarterly | Corporate tax instalments | ⚡ instalment dates |

## Chef de projet
| Period | Work | System support |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Approve requests for his projects; answer tickets | 🏠 « À approuver », 🏠 « Mes tickets » |
| Weekly | Project money view (spent, committed, floats on his sites) | 🏠 project card |
| Monthly | Situations / décomptes follow-up (later modules) | ⚡ décompte deadlines (later) |

## Chef de chantier / détenteur de caisse (site cash holder)
| Period | Work | System support |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Record bons de caisse (photo + amount + category); confirm cash received (décharge) | 🏠 big buttons « Bon de caisse », « Confirmer réception »; float remaining shown |
| Weekly (or when low) | Send relevé de caisse | 🏠 « Envoyer mon relevé », ⚡ float below 20 % |
| Monthly | Cash count with the controller | ⚡ count due |

## Achats / magasin
| Period | Work | System support |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Create BC, receive BL, record bons de retour | 🏠 quick actions |
| Weekly | Factures to match (BC/BL/facture) | 🏠 « Factures à rapprocher », ⚡ facture without BL |
| Monthly | Supplier balances | Grid view |

## Comptable externe (read-only)
| Period | Work | System support |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | Export the month (bank, cash, documents) | 🏠 « Export du mois » |
| Yearly | Year-end package | Exports |

## Home page rule
Each role's home shows **only** its tiles, ordered: overdue first, then today, then this week.
Each tile = icon + label + count + age of the oldest item; one click opens the filtered list.
Periodic tasks (weekly/monthly/…) appear as tiles only when due (configurable lead time).
